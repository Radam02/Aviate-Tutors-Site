About Aviate Tutors

Thoughtful tutoring, grounded in real-world problem-solving.

Aviate Tutors brings a structured, one-on-one approach to high school math and SAT/ACT preparation. Our focus is helping students understand the “why” behind the steps, build useful habits, and become more independent problem-solvers.

Tutoring is led by an aerospace engineering graduate from Georgia Tech, private pilot, and practicing civil engineer, with additional experience in test engineering and the software industry. Outside of work, interests include running, cooking, and playing guitar in a band.

That mix of technical work and creative pursuits shapes an approach that values preparation, patience, curiosity, and clear communication.
